FAQs

Common Questions

Can I pay in instalments?

Yes! We offer flexible payment arrangements. Speak to our admin team at either branch to set up a plan that works for you. A minimum deposit is required to confirm enrolment.

How long does it take to get my license?

Most students complete their Standard Package and pass their road test within 6–10 weeks, depending on lesson frequency and individual progress. Our AI readiness score will tell you exactly when you're ready.

Do lessons expire if I don't use them all?

Each package has a validity period (shown above). Unused lessons within the validity period can be extended by contacting admin. We always accommodate genuine scheduling challenges.

Can I switch instructors if I'm not comfortable?

Absolutely. Your comfort is our priority. You can request an instructor change at any time through the student portal or by calling the branch directly. No questions asked.

What happens if I fail my road test?

Don't worry — it happens! Our AI score ensures we only recommend you for testing when you're truly ready. If you do fail, we offer discounted top-up lessons to get you across the line. Your success is our success.

Not Sure Which Package? Let Us Help.

Call us or fill in our contact form and we'll recommend the right package based on your driving experience and goals.
