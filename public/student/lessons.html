📲 QR Code Attendance

How QR Attendance Works

1

Arrive at the branch for your scheduled lesson.

2

Your instructor will show you a QR code at the start of the lesson. You cannot scan it from home!

3

Open your phone's camera or QR scanner and scan the code at the branch.

4

Your attendance is automatically recorded — no paper, no fuss!

⏰

You must be at the branch to scan the QR code. The QR code is only valid for a limited time after your lesson starts.
